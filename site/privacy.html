Privacy

This website collects almost nothing. The product keeps customer data on infrastructure its operator controls.

Draft. This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not take effect until NEXORA publishes it as final. The entity that operates OmniFlow is to be named here before launch.

This website

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. No cookies are set.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • Our web server keeps ordinary access logs (IP address, time, page) for security and capacity, for a limited period.

The product

  • OmniFlow runs on a host its operator chooses. Workflow definitions, run history, secrets and the audit log are stored there, with secrets encrypted at rest.
  • Within a NEXORA organisation, OmniFlow sends NEXORA usage counts (such as runs started and finished) and audit events (such as a workflow being published). It does not send step inputs, outputs or secrets.
  • Each tenant's data is kept apart from every other tenant's.

Your rights and contact

To ask about, correct or delete personal data, contact NEXORA through its contact page.
