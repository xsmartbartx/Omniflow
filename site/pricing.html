Everything, not a trial

Approvals, rollback, the audit log and dry runs are in every plan, including Free.

No overage charges

At the limit, new runs are refused with a clear message until the next period or an upgrade. Dry runs still work.

No seats

An organisation pays for runs, not for the number of people who review or approve them.

Questions

Pricing, answered.

What counts as a run?

Each real (non-dry) run that is started counts once. Dry runs and retries of individual steps do not.

How do I pay?

Through your NEXORA organisation's billing. Constans does not take payment separately.

Can I try it before paying?

The Free plan includes every capability within its monthly allowance.
